# Vrinda-Store-Annual-Report-2022(Interactive Dashboard created using MS Excel)

## PROJECT OBJECTIVES

- Analyze Vrinda Store's annual sales performance.
- Monitor key sales and order KPIs.
- Compare sales between men and women customers.
- Analyze monthly sales and order trends.
- Identify the top-performing states based on sales.
- Analyze order status and customer segments.
- Identify the most popular sales channels.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns through data visualization.

- ## Dataset used

- <a href="https://github.com/Amrusha25/VRINDA-SALES-ANNUAL-REPORT-2022/blob/main/VRINDA%20STORE%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%202022.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)

- Compare sales and orders with respect to month.

- Compare sales between men and women customers.

- Analyze the number of orders based on order status.

- Find the top 5 states with respect to sales.

- Analyze sales based on age group and gender.

- Compare the number of orders received through different sales channels.

- Identify the sales channel contributing the highest number of orders.

- Analyze overall customer purchasing patterns.

## Project Insights

- The dashboard analyzes Vrinda Store's sales and order data for the year 2022.
- Women customers contribute a higher share of sales compared to men customers.
- The monthly analysis helps identify variations in sales and order volume throughout the year.
- Maharashtra has the highest sales among the top-performing states, followed by Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh.
- The majority of orders have been successfully delivered.
- Amazon contributes the highest number of orders among the different sales channels.
